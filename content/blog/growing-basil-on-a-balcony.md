---
title: "Growing basil on a balcony"
description: "How to keep balcony basil bushy and green all summer."
date: "2026-08-14"
---
Basil needs at least six hours of direct sun. A south- or west-facing balcony is ideal.

## Pot and soil

Use an eight-inch pot with drainage and a light potting mix with compost.

## Watering

Water when the top inch of soil is dry. Wet roots rot.

## Pinch the flowers

Pinch off flower buds as soon as they appear so the plant keeps making leaves.
