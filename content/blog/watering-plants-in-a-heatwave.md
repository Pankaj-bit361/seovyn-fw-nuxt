---
title: "Watering plants in a heatwave"
description: "Keep potted plants alive when temperatures spike."
date: "2026-09-02"
---
The most common mistake in a heatwave is watering at midday, when water evaporates before it reaches the roots.

## Water early

Water in the first two hours after sunrise.

## Mulch

A layer of bark or straw on top of the soil keeps moisture in.

## Check with a finger

Push a finger an inch into the soil. Water only if it's dry there too.
